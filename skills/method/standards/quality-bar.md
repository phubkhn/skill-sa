# Core Quality Bar

Apply these rules proportionally to every architecture answer and artifact:

- The problem, outcome, and scope are clear.
- Claims about the current system cite evidence; unsupported claims are assumptions.
- Important drivers are measurable or explicitly open.
- Consequential recommendations compare credible alternatives.
- Negative consequences and reversibility are visible.
- Names and ownership are consistent across artifacts.
- Boundaries state direction, purpose, contract, and failure behaviour.
- Security, resilience, observability, cost direction, and delivery impact are considered.
- Open questions have an owner or a clear next action.
- The result is concise enough for its audience to review.

Specialised artifact checklists live in their owning standards. Report failures and material omissions; do not print a large pass matrix unless the user asks for it.
